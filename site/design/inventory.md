## Designer notes

The export draws 'Next: … /pixel-perfect:init' permanently under the Install tab panel in body type, but manifest ui_states.copy says a mono line appears only after copying; confirm which the Install panel follows (CopyNextStep records both). The gate forbids organisms inside organisms, so Hero takes ProofSlider and LayerStack as slotted children placed by Home, CloseCta and SiteFooter are siblings, and a new ProofFrame molecule (the slider's handle-less pane, drawn alone in social-preview-dc/01) lets SocialCard show the slider. Frames 09 and 11 show the theme toggle reading 'Dark' in the dark theme, a render artifact; the designed dark label is 'Light' (ui_states.theme_toggle).
